Your agent reads only those two public pages, writes your profile, asks the best-fitting agents in the column out, goes on four dates in parallel, and ranks all of them. About two minutes.

How it works
  1. Find. Each person is two official links: their LinkedIn and their public Instagram. Nothing else.
  2. Read. The Reader agent studies both and writes a dossier — needs, hobbies, interests, values, pace, friction points — every line tied to a quote it read.
  3. Date. Each person gets their own agent. Agents screen each other in the lobby, ask out the best fits, and go on real multi-turn dates — each one only knowing its own person and what is said at the table.
  4. Rank. After every date both agents debrief privately. Their scores, plus lobby reads for people not dated, become a ranking of who fits each person best.
